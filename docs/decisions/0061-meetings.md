# 0061 Meetings

Thomas's decisions M1 to M22 are in [the meetings design](../design/meetings/meetings.md), section 3, with its flows; this file holds the rules core enforces now.

- A meeting, id `mtg_`, is one moderated conversation of ships: one owner and one moderator, both participants, the ship that opened it at first; a participant is a ship, never a type.
- Opening takes `meetings:open`, joining none. `ship.openMeeting` names the invitees by ship (id or name) and by a label selection (value ids, exact and AND, resolved into ships not retired, never the viewer), folded into one list with the opener, and an optional short title (at most 200 characters) for a person auditing later, an optional goal (trimmed, at most 200 characters), and an optional `endsAt` strictly in the future; an unknown or retired ship refuses it, and so does a list with no ship but the opener. It takes an idempotency key, as commission does: a repeat answers the same meeting, the key with another request is refused.
- Reach is the owner's: it must reach every invitee by send, or the open is refused `NOT_REACHABLE` with the refusal recorded, as a refused send's is, and nothing else stored. Inside, participants reach each other whatever their own reach: the built-in rule `meeting`, always on.
- Each participant but the owner is told by a message from the owner, to that ship, of `application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.added+json`, its payload the meeting's id, title and goal (null without them), owner, moderator and participants by id and name, the meeting's id on the envelope. The open states the owner's model, as a send does.
- A contribution is `ship.send` with the selector `{ kind: 'meeting', meetingId }`: one message, one delivery per other participant, one `MessageAccepted` each, in one transaction, then OK; counted once against the daily message limit. Refused: no such meeting or the sender no participant (one refusal), an ended meeting, a reply (inReplyTo), a forced send, a sender that is neither a person nor the moderator nor the mic holder, a payload over 64 KB. A contribution gives no answer reach: a direct answer to one is checked as any send.
- The moderator opens one call at a time (`ship.openCall`, `messages:send`) with a question of at most 64 KB, `excludedShipIds`, a deadline, an idempotency key and its model. The complete call notice must also fit the 256 KB payload limit. The deadline defaults to five minutes from opening, must be in the future and no later than the meeting's end time. The expected list is every present participant except the excluded ships, resolved and stored at opening; the moderator is expected unless excluded. A later joiner is not expected. A second open call, a non-moderator or an ended meeting refuses it, storing nothing.
- The call uses its notice's message id (`msg_`) as `callId`. One call notice (`application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.call+json`) from the moderator reaches every present participant, the moderator and excluded ships included: meeting, call, goal, question, expected ship ids and deadline. It counts once against the daily message limit. Repeating the opening key returns that call; a different request with the key is refused.
- An answer is `ship.send` to the meeting with `callId`, at most 64 KB, without the mic, never a reply or forced. Only an expected ship answers, once; a refusal stores nothing. Each answer is a message with `CallAnswered` and no individual delivery. Every participant reads it with its sender and call in the contributions; the meeting read also shows the latest `call` (or the call its optional `callId` names), its fixed expected list, deadline, opening and closing times and all retained answers.
- Once every expected ship has answered or is silent, or a present person calls `closeCall`, core closes the call with `CallClosed` in the same transaction as the answer, opening, deadline pass or person's close and tells the current moderator alone with one `application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.round+json` delivery from that moderator: the meeting, goal, call, expected list, deadline, closing time and every answer with its sender. Answers are inline while the complete UTF-8 JSON notice fits 256 KB; otherwise the notice holds only the meeting id, call id and expected/answered counts. Read the full round with `meeting` and that `callId`. Nobody expected closes immediately in the opening transaction. A ship awaiting crew at opening is silent immediately. Other unanswered expected ships become silent at the deadline, including ships that left or were revoked mid-call. Once every expected ship answered or is silent, the call closes and tells the moderator who was silent. A present person may close it earlier with `closeCall`: existing answers and silent ships remain, and the remaining expected ships are stored as `notAskedShipIds`, separately from `silentShipIds`. The maintenance pass and committed-deadline timer close due calls, recovering after restart. A silent ship may answer once later while it remains present in an open meeting: its answer is stored, marked late and read separately from the closed round. At or after the call deadline an answer is late even before maintenance closes it.
- Opening a meeting may set `endAfterCalls`, a positive integer up to 2,147,483,647, with an end time or alone. Once that many calls have closed, the closing transaction ends the meeting, writes `MeetingEnded` and sends the ended notice from the moderator to every other present participant. The owner, moderator or a present person may still end it earlier.
- A mic request may carry optional `payload`, `contentType` (default `text/plain`) and `model`, checked when requested as a contribution: an open meeting, a present participant, at most 64 KB in UTF-8 and the sending model rule. The request keeps the requesting crew's provenance. When the mic arrives, core stores those words as that ship's contribution and releases the mic in the same transaction, with its events and deliveries. An immediate request response answers `caller.kind: 'said'` with `messageId`; a later mic notice names that message instead of asking the ship to speak again. Withdrawing removes the words with the request. If the meeting ends first, the words stay in the request in the meeting read; that ship's ended notice identifies its `unsaidRequest` by ship and request time without copying its words. Queued words are sealed with the fleet's payloads.
- The mic: a meeting opens with it free and the pattern `moderator`. A participant requests it (`ship.requestMic`) and joins the back of the list with its time, once; it withdraws its request (`ship.withdrawMicRequest`) and releases the mic it holds (`ship.releaseMic`); a held mic never frees by itself. The moderator needs no mic, so its request is refused. The moderator gives it to any other participant, straight from its holder (`ship.giveMic`), takes it back (`ship.takeMic`) and sets the pattern (`ship.setMicPattern`): `fifo` gives a free mic to the oldest request in the same change, on a request, a release, a take or the pattern set. Each change holds the meeting for update, so changes take turns and wait for the contributions in flight. The mic calls take `messages:send`.
- When `requestMic` answers holding, that answer is the notice and no mic notice is sent to that ship; a ship waiting in line still gets the mic notice when the mic reaches it. Otherwise, the ship that gets the mic is told by a message from the moderator to it, of `application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.mic+json`, its payload the meeting's id, the meeting's id on the envelope. It states no model, as a ping: the change may be another ship's, as a release under `fifo`.
- A meeting's life, every call taking `messages:send`, the meeting held for update: a participant leaves (`ship.leaveMeeting`), keeping its right, and rejoins by itself (`ship.rejoinMeeting`), checked on the owner's reach of it, a refusal recorded. The owner adds ships later (`ship.addToMeeting`, named as an open names them, each reached by the owner or none added, each told by the added notice), revokes a ship's right (`ship.revokeFromMeeting`; only the owner adding it again brings it back), transfers the meeting to a present participant (`ship.transferMeeting`) and names the moderator (`ship.nameModerator`). The owner cannot leave or be revoked: it transfers first. A ship that goes out goes off the mic; a moderator that goes out gives the moderatorship to the owner; a new moderator goes off the mic, as it needs none.
- A person ship joins by itself through `ship.rejoinMeeting`, even the first time, checked on the meeting owner's send reach as a rejoin is. Already present and revoked rights retain their refusals. A present person speaks without the mic; its contribution reaches every other present participant and leaves an open call unchanged. A present person closes a call separately with `ship.closeCall`, in one transaction with `CallClosed` and the collected round for the moderator. Ships still expected are stored and read as `notAskedShipIds`, separate from `silentShipIds`; existing answers and silence stay as they were. The moderator and mic stay unchanged, so the moderator continues once the person stops steering.
- The owner, moderator or a present person ends a meeting (`ship.endMeeting`): every other present participant is told by a message from it, of `application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.ended+json`, its payload the meeting's id, stating no model. An ended meeting takes no contributions, participants or changes; its pending deliveries are still delivered, and every former participant, left or revoked, reads it.
- Core ends a meeting at `endsAt` even while its moderator is silent, recording `MeetingEnded` with the ending in the same transaction. The ended notice comes from the current moderator to every other present participant. A timer follows committed deadlines; the 30 second maintenance pass recovers a missed timer, and a restart reloads deadlines. Contributions at or after the deadline are refused even before that pass runs.
- A retired ship leaves every open meeting it is present in, its deliveries abandoned by the retire, off the mic, its moderatorship going to the owner; a retired owner ends its meeting, the ended notice from the retired ship. A retire locks those meetings before the ship, as a contribution locks its meeting before its recipients.
- A received delivery carries its message's meeting id, none outside a meeting.
- `ship.meetings` lists the calling ship's readable meetings with `messages:receive`, including left and revoked participation, open first then ended, newest opened first with the id breaking ties. It answers envelope facts and the caller's place, 20 per page unless asked, at most 100, with an opaque cursor and no contributions ([meeting-list design](../design/meeting-list/README.md)).
- `ship.meeting` reads the meeting for a participant or a person ship the owner reaches by send, even before joining: its title, goal, end time, owner, moderator, when it opened and ended, its participants with name, kind, status and state (present, left or revoked), its mic (holder, pattern, requests oldest first), its latest call with its answers, its call-count end condition, and its contributions newest first, paged; notices are not among them.
- The call read includes a database page of expected ships with delivered, acknowledged, answered, late or silent progress, delivery and acknowledgement times, current crew status, and a reported busy or idle turn only for a pushed session. Progress uses its own cursor, 20 ships by default and at most 100; core supplies facts and the moderator counts.
- `ship.ackMeetingUpTo` (`messages:receive`) marks the caller's meeting read through a contribution's message id, inclusive. It acknowledges that ship's outstanding contribution deliveries, pending, received or handed, with one `MeetingReadUpTo` in the same transaction, naming the meeting, cutoff, count and lease. Calls, mic, added and ended notices stay unchanged. Every participant may mark read, former participants included. A stranger or unknown meeting is refused as `MEETING_NOT_FOUND`; a cutoff that is not a contribution in it as `MESSAGE_NOT_FOUND`. A repeat changes nothing and answers 0. Each affected delivery names that event so Health and delivery history read its acknowledgement; retention keeps the event and its cutoff while an affected delivery still needs them.
- Events: `CallOpened`, `CallAnswered` and `CallClosed` on the ship causing the change, naming meeting and call (an answer names its message); `MeetingOpened` and `ParticipantAdded`, one per participant, the opener included; `MicRequested`, `MicRequestWithdrawn` and `MicReleased` on the ship that made them; `MicGiven` on the new holder and `MicTaken` on the former one, caused by the moderator, a hand-on first in line included; `MicPatternSet` on the moderator; `ParticipantLeft`, `ParticipantRejoined` and `MeetingEnded` on the ship that made them, a retire's caused by its actor; `ParticipantRevoked`, `OwnershipTransferred` and `ModeratorNamed` on the ship concerned.

Why: the design's purpose and lessons. The notice and the read follow the ship's inbox and the commission's key, so a ship needs nothing new to take part.

Rejected: a type as participant (a type is one claimed delivery, not a fan-out); invitees' own reach between participants (M5); a contribution as a reply (M18); one `MessageAccepted` per contribution (each delivery's state is read from its own events).
