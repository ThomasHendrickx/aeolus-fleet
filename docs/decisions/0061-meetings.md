# 0061 Meetings

Thomas's decisions M1 to M22 are in [the meetings design](../design/meetings/meetings.md), section 3, with its flows; this file holds the rules core enforces now.

- A meeting, id `mtg_`, is one moderated conversation of ships: one owner and one moderator, both participants, the ship that opened it at first; a participant is a ship, never a type.
- Opening takes `meetings:open`, joining none. `ship.openMeeting` names the invitees by ship (id or name) and by a label selection (value ids, exact and AND, resolved into ships not retired, never the viewer), folded into one list with the opener, and an optional short title (at most 200 characters) for a person auditing later; an unknown or retired ship refuses it, and so does a list with no ship but the opener. It takes an idempotency key, as commission does: a repeat answers the same meeting, the key with another request is refused.
- Reach is the owner's: it must reach every invitee by send, or the open is refused `NOT_REACHABLE` with the refusal recorded, as a refused send's is, and nothing else stored. Inside, participants reach each other whatever their own reach: the built-in rule `meeting`, always on.
- Each participant but the owner is told by a message from the owner, to that ship, of `application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.added+json`, its payload the meeting's id, title (null without one), owner, moderator and participants by id and name, the meeting's id on the envelope. The open states the owner's model, as a send does.
- A contribution is `ship.send` with the selector `{ kind: 'meeting', meetingId }`: one message, one delivery per other participant, one `MessageAccepted` each, in one transaction, then OK; counted once against the daily message limit. Refused: no such meeting or the sender no participant (one refusal), an ended meeting, a reply (inReplyTo), a forced send, a sender that neither moderates nor holds the mic, a payload over 64 KB. A contribution gives no answer reach: a direct answer to one is checked as any send.
- The mic: a meeting opens with it free and the pattern `moderator`. A participant requests it (`ship.requestMic`) and joins the back of the list with its time, once; it withdraws its request (`ship.withdrawMicRequest`) and releases the mic it holds (`ship.releaseMic`); a held mic never frees by itself. The moderator needs no mic, so its request is refused. The moderator gives it to any other participant, straight from its holder (`ship.giveMic`), takes it back (`ship.takeMic`) and sets the pattern (`ship.setMicPattern`): `fifo` gives a free mic to the oldest request in the same change, on a request, a release, a take or the pattern set. Each change holds the meeting for update, so changes take turns and wait for the contributions in flight. The mic calls take `messages:send`.
- The ship that gets the mic is told by a message from the moderator to it, of `application/vnd.aeolus.meeting.mic+json`, its payload the meeting's id, the meeting's id on the envelope. It states no model, as a ping: the change may be another ship's, as a release under `fifo`.
- A received delivery carries its message's meeting id, none outside a meeting.
- `ship.meeting` reads the meeting for a participant: its title, owner, moderator, when it opened and ended, its participants with name, kind and status, its mic (holder, pattern, requests oldest first), and its contributions newest first, paged; notices are not among them.
- Events: `MeetingOpened` and `ParticipantAdded`, one per participant, the opener included; `MicRequested`, `MicRequestWithdrawn` and `MicReleased` on the ship that made them; `MicGiven` on the new holder and `MicTaken` on the former one, caused by the moderator, a hand-on first in line included; `MicPatternSet` on the moderator.

Why: the design's purpose and lessons. The notice and the read follow the ship's inbox and the commission's key, so a ship needs nothing new to take part.

Rejected: a type as participant (a type is one claimed delivery, not a fan-out); invitees' own reach between participants (M5); a contribution as a reply (M18); one `MessageAccepted` per contribution (each delivery's state is read from its own events).
