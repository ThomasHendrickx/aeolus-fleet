# 0016 No policy

Aeolus enforces scopes exactly and adds no rules of its own on top (no limits on who may create what, no safety nets). Whatever a scope allows is allowed. Do not add protective rules without the operator asking.

Why: Aeolus stays dumb and flexible; the operator decides what others may do. Extra rules add friction and surface for future issues.

Rejected: built-in guards such as "only argo may create commanding ships".
