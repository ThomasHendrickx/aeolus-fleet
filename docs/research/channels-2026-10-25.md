# Channels: a Microsoft-focused company and a small company

Research for Thomas's question of 25 Oct 2026: "we also need to check how our setup would be used in a Microsoft focussed company, and how in a small company, what channels they already use and how we can plugin". Context: [#1228](https://github.com/ThomasHendrickx/aeolus-fleet/issues/1228) (connecting technical and non-technical people) and [#1227](https://github.com/ThomasHendrickx/aeolus-fleet/issues/1227) (ships as Slack bots, PR #1224 part B). Research only: no design, no decisions. Facts are as of 25 Oct 2026; [n] points to the sources at the end. **Unconfirmed** marks a fact with no primary source, or with sources that disagree.

Effort is a rough size for Aeolus: S (configuration and a guide), M (one gateway slice), L (a new part of the gateway with its own design).

## 4. Ranked: which channels first

### Company A: Microsoft-focused

| Rank | Channel | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio over MCP in | Built. Over 30 million paid seats, no Microsoft review, credits paid by the customer. What remains is VS3's proof and a guide |
| 2 | Teams | Where the non-technical half works all day (#1228). An org catalog install needs no store review and the Teams channel costs nothing, so the cost is Aeolus's own build. Follows the shape Slack proves |
| 3 | Outlook email | The wake is built; content and answering by email would reach everyone, the IT-locked-down desktop included |
| 4 | Web push | Built; useful, but Teams covers the same moment for this company |
| Not first | Teams Workflows webhooks, Agent 365 listing | One way only, against W1; Agent 365 decided against (V2) |

### Company B: 5 to 30 people

| Rank | Channel | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Email | Built for the wake; everyone has it, including people outside the company; no vendor rules |
| 2 | ChatGPT and Claude over MCP in | Built. They already pay for one of them; one Owner or admin adds it, no review. Needs the non-technical guide (#1228) |
| 3 | Slack | Designed (0.33.0). The main chat app of small technical companies; an app outside the Marketplace installs by link, and part B's events do not hit the history limit |
| 4 | Teams | For the Microsoft 365 Business share; same work as company A's rank 2 |
| 5 | Google Chat | Sign-in is built, but serving other organisations needs the Marketplace review |
| Last | WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS | WhatsApp is where small firms talk, but a bot cannot join their groups and Meta's AI terms are a risk; Telegram and SMS are rare for staff |
