Which time zone do tide alerts use for a harbour on a border?

plannerasks · 17 Nov, 11:00

Two harbours sit on an estuary between two time zones. Their published tide tables use different zones.

  1. The harbour’s published zoneplanner’s pickAlerts match the tables the harbour master prints.Why: Harbour masters check alerts against the printed tables.
  2. The user’s own zoneAlerts match the phone, but differ from the printed tables.
