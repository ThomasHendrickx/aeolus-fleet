# 0051 Organisation (draft)

- An organisation is pagasae's tenant: its accounts, its groups (0052), its fleets and, later, its directory (0054). Every pagasae record carries its organisation, as every core record carries its fleet (0007).
- An account is a human's, by email, in one organisation. One email may hold an account in several organisations, each its own; a sign-in with such an email asks which organisation. An account is active or disabled (0055); nothing is deleted yet.
- An organisation admin is an account with the right to manage its organisation: its accounts, groups, fleets, the accounts allowed to sign in as argo of each fleet (0054) and, later, its directory. The right sits on the account, in pagasae, where no fleet is. The account created first holds it and grants it to others. The last account holding it cannot be disabled or lose it, so an organisation is never left unmanaged. Organisation rights never imply fleet scopes: in a fleet an organisation admin has only what its groups and its own rights give there (0052).
- Sign-up creates the organisation, its first account (an organisation admin, allowed to sign in as argo of the first fleet) and its first fleet: zero fleets has zero value. pagasae creates every fleet of an organisation through the installation procedures (0020), with the request id that makes a retry safe.
- pagasae is no ship. It acts in a fleet in two ways only: it vouches for accounts with account tokens (0058), and it acts as the installation, through the same fleet calls as any actor, with the scopes argo gives the installation there (0057): commissioning and labelling person ships, releasing what a leaver crews.
- An agent manages the organisation once pagasae has an MCP server: an organisation admin gives organisation rights to an agent ship of the organisation's fleets, which core vouches for with a short token pagasae checks. Until then, organisation rights are accounts'. A fleet without pagasae has argo as its way in.
- A human from another organisation, such as a contractor, gets an account in the organisation that hires them, disabled when the work ends. No guests.
- pagasae keeps beside the organisation only what is hosted: the terms accepted, updates opt-in, the installation admin's pages.

Why: "an organisation manages the user accounts within the whole. Pagasae is the account management unit." "The account created first is the organisation's admin and manages the organisation." pagasae is not a ship (Thomas, 19 Oct); it goes across fleets, so it acts as the installation (round 4, question 4).

Rejected: organisation rights held by a fleet or by every argo; guests; pagasae as a plugin ship in every fleet; new installation procedures for per-fleet work, which no ship could call; sign-up without a fleet.
