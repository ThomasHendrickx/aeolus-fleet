# 0051 Organisation (draft)

- An organisation is pagasae's tenant: its accounts, its groups (0052), its fleets and, later, its directory (0054). Every pagasae record carries its organisation, as every core record carries its fleet (0007).
- An account is a human's, by email, in one organisation. One email may hold an account in several organisations, each its own; a sign-in with such an email asks which organisation. An account is active or disabled (0055); nothing is deleted yet.
- An organisation admin is an account with the right to manage its organisation: its accounts, groups, fleets, the accounts allowed to sign in as argo of each fleet (0054) and, later, its directory. The right sits on the account, in pagasae: the one place a right is not a scope, because no fleet is there. The account created first holds it, and grants it to others. The last account holding it cannot be disabled or lose it (P9).
- Sign-up creates the organisation, its first account (an organisation admin, allowed to sign in as argo of the first fleet) and its first fleet: zero fleets has zero value. pagasae creates every fleet of an organisation through the installation procedures (0020), with the request id that makes a retry safe, and connects itself to it (below).
- In each fleet of the organisation, pagasae acts as a ship: its plugin ship, kind `plugin`, owned by argo, connected when pagasae creates the fleet, or by argo's Connect when pagasae is added to a fleet that exists (self-hosted, 0054). It holds the scopes groups may give (argo picks them at connect; all of them on a fleet pagasae created), and `fleet:person`, `scopes:grant`, `labels:define`, `labels:assign`, `fleet:manage` and `fleet:on-behalf`. Every change pagasae makes in a fleet is that ship's call and leaves its event; pagasae keeps beside it which organisation admin asked. The installation token keeps fleet create, list, get and delete, and ending console sign-ins (0054, 0055).
- A human from another organisation, such as a contractor, gets an account in the organisation that hires them, disabled when the work ends. No guests.
- pagasae keeps beside the organisation only what is hosted: the terms accepted, updates opt-in, the installation admin's pages.
- Managing the organisation happens in pagasae's pages. An MCP server to manage it from outside may come later.

Why: "an organisation manages the user accounts within the whole. Pagasae is the account management unit." Organisation management works on its own, without the gateway or an identity provider. "The account created first is the organisation's admin and manages the organisation." pagasae acting in a fleet as a ship keeps "every caller is a ship" (0012) and "no hidden behaviour": its changes show in the fleet's events like any plugin's.

Rejected: organisation rights held by a fleet or by every argo; guests from another organisation (they grow unseen); pagasae acting in a fleet through the installation token for everything (a caller that is not a ship, its changes without an acting ship); sign-up without a fleet; a set of organisation admin rights before a second one is needed (0013).
