# 0052 Groups, an account's rights and its person ship (draft)

- A group is a set of an organisation's accounts, kept in pagasae (0051). An account sits in any number of groups; an organisation admin makes groups and puts accounts in them.
- In each fleet, a group gives a set of scopes and a reach. Rights come only from groups: a right for one account is a group of one (round 5, question 2).
- **An account's rights in a fleet** are the scopes of all its groups there, carried in its account token (0058), with the values of the label `group` for those groups. A change of groups lands at the account's next token, within 10 minutes. Nothing in the fleet is rescoped for it.
- **Reach.** The installation defines the label `group` in each fleet, one value per group that gives anything there (round 5, question 1), and declares each group's reach as rules on that label (0037): "engineering sends to engineering" is a send rule from `group=engineering` to `group=engineering`. A rule joining a group to another label, such as "sales sends to the CRM" (`group=sales` to `crm=app`), is a rule in the networking plugin. A rule's selector matches an account by the values in its token, as it matches a ship by its labels (0057). The label's limits (50 values, 20 on one actor, 0031) cap the groups giving anything in one fleet at 50 and one account's at 20.
- **The person ship** is for messaging only. An account has one in a fleet when one of its groups lets it message there, or when it first owns a ship there (round 5, question 7), at most one, linked to it; the installation commissions it then (`fleet:person`), owner argo, with `messages:send` and `messages:receive`, and the values of `group` its account carries, so its messages reach where its groups do. When the account's groups change, the installation changes the person ship's labels at once. When neither holds any more, the installation releases it; it is never retired for that.
- A person ship is never recommissioned to change rights: its id, its history and the ships it owns stay.
- The fleet sees ships, scopes, labels, rules and account tokens. Groups, and which admin changed them, are pagasae's.

Why: "Accounts sit in groups, and a group yields a set of scopes and network reach; the fleet only knows ships." With the actor model (0057), an account carries its rights in its token, so a group change needs no ship changed, and a person ship is what messaging needs. The research of `accounts.md` question 4 holds: the user stays and its rights change in place.

Rejected: rights on one account beside its groups; groups in core; a selector by group; a person ship for every account with rights; rescoping person ships for rights other than messaging; retiring and recommissioning a person ship to change its rights.
