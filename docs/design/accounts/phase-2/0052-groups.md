# 0052 Groups and a person ship's rights (draft)

- A group is a set of an organisation's accounts, kept in pagasae (0051). An account sits in any number of groups. An organisation admin makes groups and puts accounts in them.
- In each fleet, a group gives a set of scopes and a reach. Rights come only from groups: a right for one account is a group of one (P3).
- An account has at most one person ship in each fleet, linked to it. It has one once a group of it gives anything in that fleet: pagasae commissions it then (`fleet:person`, 0053), owner argo, and links the account to it. Later, a group may allow it at the account's first sign-in instead, still on an organisation admin's allowance.
- The person ship's scopes are `messages:send` and `messages:receive` and the scopes of every group of its account in that fleet. pagasae grants them; a ship grants only what it holds, so a group gives only scopes pagasae's ship holds (0046).
- Reach: pagasae defines the label `group` in each fleet, one value per group that gives anything there, and assigns its person ships the values of their account's groups (P2). Each group's reach is a list of network rules on that label, which pagasae declares (0037) and the networking plugin puts in force with the rest (0035): "engineering sends to engineering" is a send rule from `group=engineering` to `group=engineering`. pagasae declares only rules whose every term is on its own label (0037); a rule joining a group to another label, such as "sales sends to the CRM" (`group=sales` to `crm=app`), is a rule in the networking plugin, which selects by any label. The label's limits (50 values, 20 on a ship, 0031) cap the groups giving anything in one fleet at 50 and one account's at 20; the refusal names them.
- A change rescopes the person ship in place, at once: an account joining or leaving a group, or a group's scopes or reach changing, changes the person ships of the accounts concerned in every fleet, with `ScopesChanged` and the label's events, effective at each ship's next call (0046). A live session does not hold it up; a call that needed a removed scope is refused, as after any scope change. At every sign-in (0054) pagasae checks the ship's scopes and labels against its groups and puts right what differs, so a change pagasae could not make at once (core unreachable) lands by then at the latest.
- A person ship is never recommissioned to change its rights: its id, its history and the ships it owns stay.
- The fleet sees ships, scopes, labels and rules only. Groups, and which admin changed them, are pagasae's.

Why: "Accounts sit in groups, and a group yields a set of scopes and network reach; the fleet only knows ships, so from the fleet's side an account has one person ship." Entra, Google Workspace, Okta, Slack and GitHub all keep the user and change its rights in place when a group changes, and push a change rather than waiting for a sign-in (`accounts.md` part 2.2). Core already rescopes a live ship at its next call. A label is how reach already selects ships (0034), so groups need no new selector.

Rejected: rights on one account beside its groups (two places to look for one human's rights); groups in core (core knows ships); a selector by group in network rules (a second way to select beside labels); rescoping only at sign-in, or only with no session attached (a removed right lingers while the human stays signed in); retiring and recommissioning a person ship to change its rights (a new ship id, its history behind, its ships falling to argo).
