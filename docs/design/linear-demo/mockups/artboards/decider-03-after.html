Which time zone do tide alerts use for a harbour on a border?

plannerasks · 17 Nov, 11:02

Saltmarsh and Eastholm sit on one estuary, either side of a time zone line. Each harbour master prints tide tables in their own zone. Slice 4 sends the first alerts, so the choice is needed before it starts.

  1. The harbour’s published zoneplanner’s pickAlerts match the tables the harbour master prints.Why: harbour masters and skippers check alerts against the printed tables.
  2. The user’s own zoneAlerts match the phone, but differ from the printed tables.
