## Real parts and reproducibility

The product inside each tour is rendered from the checked-out packages, not drawn: the console's ConsoleFrame, ListPage, DetailPage, Harbour, ShipHeader, ShipOwner, ShipTimeline, MessageThreads and NoticeBanner; Bridge's question through the console's QuestionReader (as approved M15/M18) and its messages through MessageBody; Bridge's shell from the ui package's AppHeader, Badge, HarbourTile and ToastCard, laid out as the approved [Bridge design](../../bridge/README.md) draws it. Only the agent's terminal is simulated. The data is fabricated in `fixtures.js`: Tidewater (Decider), Cedar Workshop with invoice 1042 (Flow owner) and Kestrel Studio (Builder), fictional throughout.

The site around it follows the live marketing site: `brand.css` tokens, Bricolage Grotesque headlines, Geist text, Geist Mono for ship names and times, pill buttons, the browser frame of its demo section and the navy sea panel with its fleet scene (`site.jsx`, `tour.css`). The fonts are self-hosted under `artboards/fonts` with their licences.

The mockup sources are documentation tooling, not a site implementation. To regenerate from the repository root after `npm ci`: `MOCKUP_TOOLS=<directory with @tailwindcss/cli> node docs/design/linear-demo/mockups/build.js`, then `node docs/design/linear-demo/mockups/capture.js` for the previews and checks. Generated HTML stays useful without installing anything.

## Visual checks

These checks validate rendered fixtures, not production behavior. No production tests were changed or run.
