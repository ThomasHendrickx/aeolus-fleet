SquadronTag

How squadron things are named. SquadronTag links to the squadron. A member name keeps its squadron prefix, muted, everywhere except inside its own squadron, where the part after the colon is enough; the full name is always in title and copy. Blueprints show name and version; templates name@version in mono. The flagship carries a kind chip, like argo.
