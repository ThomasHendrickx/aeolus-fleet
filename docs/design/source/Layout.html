Layout, sizes and motion

Tailwind default breakpoints, used at two points only: sm (640) and lg (1024). Fixed sizes for chrome, controls, overlays and icons; motion is short and never required to understand a change. Under prefers-reduced-motion, nothing slides or pulses.

--breakpoint-lg1024 pxDesktop: 256 px Sidebar, Header, 32 px page padding
