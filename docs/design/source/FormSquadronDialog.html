FormSquadronDialog

Forming takes two steps in one Dialog: pick a blueprint and version, then preview what gets commissioned. Confirming opens the new squadron’s page, Forming, where each member shows its crew line and launch note and the progress fills as members check in. Phone: a full-screen page per step.
