AccountMenu

Everything about the person signed in: who, this session, account settings, theme, sign out. Future account features go here, never into page chrome. Desktop: the account button at the foot of Sidebar opens a DropdownMenu upwards. Phone: an Avatar button at the right end of TopBar on every root page opens the same items in a bottom Sheet. Sign out needs no confirm. The session line matters because signing in on another device ends this one.
