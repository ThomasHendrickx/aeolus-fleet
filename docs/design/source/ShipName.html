ShipName

Ship names are handles: lowercase letters, digits, hyphens and colons, always in the sans family at weight 500. The 4-character id suffix appears where a name may be ambiguous over time: timelines, delivery history, retired ships. argo never has a suffix and reads “argo (you)” where the operator is a party.
