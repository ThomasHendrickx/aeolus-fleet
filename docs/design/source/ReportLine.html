ReportLine

What a ship says it is doing: Working, Blocked or Idle, a short note and when it reported. Every ship can report; argo never does. The state word and icon take the tone colour, the note stays --foreground, the time is muted. Rows truncate the note and keep the time; FleetTable drops the time to the title, LastSeen carries recency there.
