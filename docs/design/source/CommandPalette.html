CommandPalette

Desktop ⌘K palette: actions, ships (with StatusBadge), pages. Arrow keys move, Enter opens, Escape closes. argo is not listed as a ship; its inbox is under Go to. With squadrons on it adds Form squadron, squadrons with their state, Squadrons under Go to, blueprints, and actions on a matched squadron (add member, upgrade, stand down). Phone: the search icon in TopBar opens the same content full screen.
