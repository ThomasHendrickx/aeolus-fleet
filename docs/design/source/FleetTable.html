FleetTable

The fleet overview list. argo sorts first with the operator kind chip and never reports. Every other ship shows its report: state, note, time. Crewed ships show a compact LocationTag, awaiting ships their prompt status. With squadrons on, flagships carry the flagship kind chip, member names keep their squadron prefix muted (so a squadron sorts together) and a Squadron filter joins the toolbar. With squadrons off none of this shows. On phone each ship becomes a row of up to four lines.
