LastSeen

When the fleet last heard from the ship’s session, as an icon only: full signal under 1 minute, medium signal from 1 to 15 minutes, low signal after 15 minutes. Shape changes with recency, colour only supports it (ok tone, muted, waiting tone). The exact “Last seen 25 s ago” is the accessible name and shows in a Tooltip on desktop hover. Same thresholds for every ship, squadron members included; member health stays on the squadron page. No icon for ships without a session.
