ShipHeader

Top of the ship page: name, status, type or kind, the main actions, the ship’s latest report and a meta strip with location, age, fleet scopes and id. Actions follow state: awaiting ships lead with Get starting prompt; crewed ships with Message; argo shows only Open inbox and Copy ship id; retired ships are read-only. With squadrons on, a member adds a squadron strip (squadron, role and template, health, hand-offs), leads with Get new crew line when it is silent, and has no Rename or Retire; a flagship explains itself and links to its squadron.
